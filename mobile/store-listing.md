# TradePilot AI — Play Store Listing

## App Name
TradePilot AI

## Short Description
AI-powered trading strategy builder, backtester, and auto-trader.

## Full Description
TradePilot AI helps you build, backtest, and automate trading strategies with artificial intelligence.

**Build Strategies Visually**
- Drag-and-drop strategy builder with indicators (RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, SMA, EMA)
- Set entry/exit conditions with a visual node editor
- Define stop loss, take profit, and risk management parameters

**Extract Strategies from YouTube**
- Paste any trading YouTube video link
- AI extracts the strategy from the video description
- Auto-fill your strategy builder with extracted indicators and rules

**Backtest with Real Market Data**
- Run backtests on historical data (crypto, forex, commodities, indices)
- See equity curves, win rates, Sharpe ratio, and drawdown
- Walk-forward parameter optimization

**Convert to PineScript**
- Generate TradingView PineScript v5 code from your strategies
- Copy-paste directly into TradingView
- Use TradingView alerts to trigger signals

**Auto-Trade**
- Connect your Binance account (paper trading by default)
- Bot monitors markets and executes trades automatically
- Risk caps: max position size, daily loss limit, cooldown periods

**Push Notifications**
- Get alerts when your strategy conditions are met
- Real-time push notifications on your phone
- Toggle alerts on/off per strategy

**Paper Trading First**
- All trades start in paper mode
- No real money until you explicitly enable live trading
- Full risk management built in

**Security**
- JWT authentication
- Encrypted API keys
- HTTPS everywhere
